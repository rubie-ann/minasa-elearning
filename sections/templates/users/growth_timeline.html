Planting & Early Growth

1–2 Months

The journey begins as arrowroot rhizomes are planted in well-prepared, moist soil. Healthy, disease-free planting materials ensure good establishment.

Do: Keep the soil moist and shaded to help new shoots develop strong roots. Don’t: Plant during heavy rains — excess water can cause rotting or stunted growth.
